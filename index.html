The world gets the surface. This is what lives underneath.

There are things I can explain in conversation. There are things I can joke about. And then there are things that only seem to exist when everything gets quiet.

01 the surface

The person everyone sees.

I can look fine. I can joke. I can talk about ordinary things and carry myself like nothing is wrong.

There is a strange skill in learning how to keep pain behind a normal face.

People see the surface because the surface is what I learned to show them.

02 the mask

What I don't say out loud.

I've spent years feeling like there is something fundamentally wrong with me. Like being ordinary means being forgettable. Like someone will eventually find someone better and leave.

That fear follows me into relationships, friendships, conversations, silence — almost everything.

“I don't want to be everyone's temporary person. I want someone to choose me and stay.” — something I wish were easier to say
03 loneliness

It gets quiet here.

There are nights when the silence feels enormous.

I have so much affection, loyalty, protection and care inside me, and nowhere safe to put it. I can give and give and still end up watching people disappear.

Sometimes I don't need advice. I just want someone to stay.

03:17 AM / fragment
Sometimes the hardest part isn't being alone.

It's having so much love in you and nowhere for it to go.
04 abandonment

Being replaced.

Abandonment is not always someone walking away. Sometimes it is the constant expectation that they eventually will.

It can make reassurance feel like oxygen. It can make distance feel dangerous. It can make a small change in someone's tone feel much larger than it really is.

I'm trying to learn that love does not have to be held together by fear.

I don't actually want to spend my life asking whether someone is going to leave.

I want to know what it feels like when staying is simply natural.

05 tenderness

I want to be held.

Not because I am incapable of standing on my own.

Because sometimes being strong for too long makes you want somewhere soft to finally put the weight down.

I want warmth. A hand held without having to ask. An embrace that says, without words, you don't have to carry everything right now.

There is nothing shameful about wanting tenderness.

the thing I never learned
You can be strong
and still want somewhere to rest your head.
06 what hurt

I remember being made to feel small.

Being quiet. Looking different. Not fitting the mold. Being mocked for things I could not simply switch off.

After enough years, other people's words can become your own voice.

I'm still learning how to separate what happened to me from what I believe about myself.

07 faith

I still believe.

My faith matters to me. Christ matters to me. So does discipline, honesty, loyalty and trying to become a better man.

That does not mean I am never exhausted. It does not mean I never question whether I can keep carrying something.

Faith is not pretending the darkness isn't there. Sometimes it is choosing to keep walking through it.

still
Even here,
God is still here.
08 underneath

The boy underneath the man.

08.01

He wants reassurance.

Not because he wants to be controlled, but because being cared for feels unfamiliar and precious.

08.02

He wants somewhere safe.

A person, a room, a moment where he doesn't have to perform strength.

08.03

He wants to matter.

Not as someone's project. Not as a temporary distraction. As someone worth choosing.

08.04

He is still here.

And maybe that is more important than he gives himself credit for.

END OF THE LETTER

Maybe being unseen
isn't the same as being unworthy.

I don't know what the future looks like. I don't know who stays. I only know that there is still a part of me that hopes someone will eventually see the whole thing — and not turn away.

— still here. still hoping.
